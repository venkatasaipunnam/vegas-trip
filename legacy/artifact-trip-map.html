Day by day

Sunday Oct 25 · open 8 AM – 6 PM

Universal Studios, no scary rides

Universal Hollywood is built around a studio tour, shows and gentle dark rides, so you can have a full morning without a single coaster. Plan to be inside by about 9:20 and leave by 3:00 so Hollywood and the Santa Monica sunset still fit. The park closes at 6 PM for Halloween Horror Nights, a separate scare event. You will be gone long before it starts.

Your route through the park

  1. 9:20Studio Tour first. The park's signature: a ~60-minute tram ride through real backlots and sets. A few staged moments (a small quake, a flash flood, King Kong in 3D, a Jaws scare) but nothing that drops or spins. Lines grow fast, so do it first.
  2. 10:30The Secret Life of Pets: Off the Leash. A gentle, cute dark ride.
  3. 11:00Wizarding World of Harry Potter. Walk Hogsmeade, see the wand-choosing show at Ollivanders, photograph Hogwarts castle, drink a Butterbeer.
  4. 12:15Lunch at Three Broomsticks (Harry Potter) or Krusty Burger in Springfield. Catch the WaterWorld stunt show if a showtime fits (check the app; front rows get soaked).
  5. 1:15Starway escalators to the Lower Lot. About 10 minutes down, with views over the valley.
  6. 1:30Super Nintendo World. Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge is an augmented-reality kart ride with turns but no drops. Buy or borrow a Power-Up Band to play the wall games and collect coins.
  7. 2:30Back up for Kung Fu Panda Adventure in the DreamWorks Theatre (a film with gently moving seats), then walk out through Springfield.
  8. 3:00Exit and walk down to Universal City/Studio City station.

Use the official Universal Studios Hollywood app for live wait times and show times. If your ticket includes Express or Early Access, you can add more and still leave on time.

Ride guide for you

Sunday Oct 25 · after Universal

Hollywood Sign & Santa Monica sunset

Is the Santa Monica sunset worth it? Yes. The sun sets straight over the Pacific at about 6:05 PM that evening, and the pier lights and the Ferris wheel switch on right as the sky turns orange. It is the best way to end a busy park day, and it is free. Get there by 5:30 to walk the pier first.
Saturday Oct 31 · your last day

Arizona: Grand Canyon West + Hoover Dam

A guided bus tour solves the no-car problem: hotel pickup around 6:30 AM, about 3 to 3.5 hours at the canyon's West Rim, a Hoover Dam photo stop, and back in Vegas around 5 to 6 PM. That leaves a calm evening before your 11:59 PM flight.

Getting around & bookings

Transit, cheapest first

The Deuce bus · Las Vegas

Double-decker that runs the whole Strip to Fremont Street, 24 hours a day. $8 for a 24-hour pass, $20 for 3 days. Buy in the rideRTC app before boarding.

Las Vegas Monorail

Runs behind the east side of the Strip, Sahara to MGM Grand, with a stop at Westgate. 24-hour pass $15 ($13.45 online). Monday it runs until midnight, Tuesday to Thursday until 2 AM, Friday to Sunday until 3 AM.

LA Metro B & E Lines

B Line: Union Station → Hollywood/Highland → Universal City. E Line: 7th St/Metro Center → Downtown Santa Monica. Tap a TAP card or your phone wallet at the gate.
